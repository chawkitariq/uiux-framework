# Premium copy and compliance

## Writing
- Talk about problems and results, never technical categories. The reader should recognise their own situation.
- State, do not justify. Short sentences, one idea each, restrained tone.
- No absolute promises ("always", "never", "guaranteed"). Re-read each phrase to check it cannot be read backwards.
- No commitment on pace, duration, place or terms on a public page.
- Avoid worn market words ("solutions", "expertise", "no commitment", "digital", "boost"...). Keep a project blocklist and extend it.
- Nothing invented: no figures, references or testimonials without a source. A mock-up that could pass for real is labelled "Example"; elsewhere it is not.
- Nothing that looks like a construction site online (brackets, "coming soon"): hide the element until the content exists.

## Compliance review (mandatory before build and before release)
A compliance agent (mid-tier model) checks every public text for: absolutes, unkeepable commitments, blocklisted terms, invented facts, private details. Its deliverable is ready-to-paste corrections, not comments.
